# Message Requests and Linked Access

<!-- doc-coverage: better_together/message_requests, better_together/person_access_grants, better_together/person_links -->

This guide covers three ways you control who can reach you or see more of your information: **message requests**, **your messaging permissions**, and **access grants and person links** that connect you with people on other, connected platforms.

You are always in charge. Nothing here is shared until you accept or grant it, and you can take it back.

## Message requests

> Message requests are an early feature. If you do not see **Send Message Request** or **Message Requests** on your platform, your platform has not switched it on yet.

When you want to talk to someone who has not allowed messages from you, you can send a short note instead of a message.

1. Choose **Send Message Request** on their profile.
2. Answer **Why would you like to connect?** Introduce yourself in up to 1000 characters.
3. Select **Send Message Request**. You can have only one pending request to the same person at a time; the button then reads **Request pending**.

The person receives your note under **Message Requests**, where it says you "would like to message you". They can:

- **Accept & Start Conversation**: a conversation opens with your note as the first message, and from then on you are allowed to message them.
- **Decline**: nothing is shared, and no conversation is created.

## Who can message you

You can be added to a conversation when any of these is true:

- you switched on **Receive messages from members** in your profile preferences;
- you accepted a message request from that person;
- you accepted an invitation from that person (accepting an invitation lets the inviter message you, without opening your inbox to everyone);
- the person is a steward of your platform.

To stop messages from the whole community, switch **Receive messages from members** off. Accepting a request or an invitation only ever allows the one person.

## Linked people on other platforms

When your platform is connected to another one, people on both sides can agree to a **person link** through an exchange request. A link shows that a specific person over there and a specific person here have agreed to recognise each other.

Open **Person Links** to see your links, the platform connection each uses, and whether it is verified. **Revoke Link** ends the link and every access grant on it.

## Access grants

An access grant says what one person may see of another's private material over a person link. Open **Access Grants** to see what you are **sharing** and what has been **shared with you**.

Each grant lists scopes, each either **Allowed** or **Denied**:

| Scope | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Profile read | Your profile |
| Private posts | Posts you keep private |
| Private pages | Pages you keep private |
| Private events | Events you keep private |
| Private messages | Messages |

A new grant allows **Profile read** only. If you are the one sharing, you can change the scopes or use **Revoke Access**, which ends the grant straight away. Grants can have an expiry date; after it the access stops.

## Your data

Message requests, messaging permissions, person links and access grants are handled when you delete your account. See [My Data and Exports](my_data_and_exports.md) and [Account Deletion Requests](account_deletion_requests.md).
